Timing notices now explain themselves. Each `[Runtime note] ⏱ v1` notice keeps its structured JSON receipt and timing fields unchanged, and adds self-contained guidance prose after them: it identifies the note as an automated harness update rather than a user message, explains what elapsed, deadline, remaining, and budget mean, and gives pacing guidance. Urgency reflects the run's current position from the receipt's cumulative milestone coverage rather than the exact trigger, so a threshold that fires late (a 90% warning at 99% elapsed) or coalesces with a periodic bucket still says "at least 90% used and 10% or less remains" with wrap-up instruction, and periodic notices after the 90% mark never revert to normal-pace wording. Runs without a deadline get elapsed-only context stating there is no deadline. The prose never claims in-flight tools are forcibly stopped at the deadline instant, matching actual behavior where an expired deadline ends the run with a result a retry cannot renew, and describes the update schedule as becoming due at intervals and thresholds and delivered at the next safe boundary between steps, with separately due updates combined into one note, rather than promising guaranteed wall-clock delivery. The compact one-line rendering in the CLI and TUI parses only the machine-readable fields segment, so display is unchanged for both new notices and legacy persisted ones without guidance; the raw line, shown in full in detail views and the Web UI, is now readable on its own.
